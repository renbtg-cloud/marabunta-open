### 2. Valuation Models

#### Model A: The Standard Tech M&A (Theoretical Maximum)
Based on traditional enterprise infrastructure multiples (10x - 15x Forward ARR).
*   **Valuation Range:** $1.2 Billion - $1.8 Billion
*   **Execution Friction:** EXTREME. Requires 12+ months due diligence, SEC/DOJ antitrust scrutiny, Board of Directors approval. High risk of "Vampire Attack" (reverse-engineering during diligence). Incompatible with "Ghost Handoff".

#### Model B: The Defensive Kill-Shot (Hyperscaler Buyout)
Hyperscaler (AWS/GCP) acquires solely to bury the IP and protect EC2 margins.
*   **Valuation Range:** $200,000,000 - $400,000,000
*   **Execution Friction:** HIGH. Requires NDAs, strict non-competes, and US regulatory oversight.

#### Model C: The "Pocket Change" Discretionary Exit (Recommended)
Priced to slip beneath board-level scrutiny and trigger immediate, unilateral execution by a Senior VP at a Tier-1 Telco or Legacy Enterprise.
*   **Valuation Range:** $35,000,000 - $50,000,000
*   **Execution Friction:** LOW. Falls within quarterly discretionary M&A budgets. Enables rapid cash settlement via offshore escrow.
