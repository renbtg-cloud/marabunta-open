Highlighted Case Study: The Research Ecosystem

Institutions like the Max Planck Institute (Germany) and ETH Zurich stand to gain the most from "Research-at-the-Edge." By bypassing months of queue time on centralized supercomputers, these labs can execute massively parallel drug-discovery or climate-modeling tasks on the global spot market. However, if rival labs in the PRC or the US adopt Marabunta first, the institution's citation index and discovery lead will collapse as competitors achieve 100x the simulation density.

Highlighted Case Study: The Sovereign FinOps

State actors like Saudi Aramco and ICBC (China) utilize Marabunta to establish national data perimeters. For these entities, the benefit is absolute sovereignty: surviving network partitions or international sanctions without losing the ability to settle internal financial transactions or manage energy routing.
